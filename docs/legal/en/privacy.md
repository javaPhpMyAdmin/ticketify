## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use Ticketify, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect the data you give us directly: your email address, your name, and the tickets and receipts you upload or scan in the app. We also store data generated by your use, such as categories, budgets, households and preferences. When you purchase the Pro subscription, we receive purchase confirmation from the app store.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

We use your data to provide the service: syncing your tickets across devices, computing your budgets and statistics, and managing your account. We also use aggregated and anonymous data to improve the app. Legal document acceptance records are kept to meet our audit obligations.

## 4. Third Parties

To operate the service we work with trusted providers: Supabase hosts the database and handles authentication; RevenueCat manages in-app subscriptions; and Apple App Store and Google Play process payments. These providers process your data only to provide their services and under our instructions.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes of this policy. Legal document acceptance records are kept immutably for audit purposes. When you delete your account, your data is erased, except where we must keep it by law.

## 6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. Deleting your account erases your tickets, budgets, households and related data. Cancelling your paid subscription is handled separately from the app store.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export and delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can export your data from the app or contact us through the channel listed below to exercise any of these rights.

## 8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or about how we process your data, email us at [contact@ticketify.app]. Please note this is a draft: the final version will be published after legal review.
