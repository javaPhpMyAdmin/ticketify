## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use Ticketify, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect the data you give us directly: your email address and your name. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email and account name from that provider. To scan receipts, you capture photos with your device's camera or pick images from your photo library; those photos are stored against your account. You can also enter tickets manually with their amount, store and category. Use of the app generates additional data: custom categories, budgets, shared households and app preferences. If you buy the Pro subscription, the app store confirms the purchase to us.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

We use your data to provide the service: syncing your tickets across devices, computing your budgets and statistics, and managing your account. If you enable biometric authentication, biometric data is processed locally on your device and never leaves it. We also use aggregated and anonymous data to improve the app. Legal document acceptance records are kept to meet our audit obligations.

## 4. Third Parties

To operate the service we work with trusted providers: Supabase hosts the database and handles authentication; RevenueCat manages in-app subscriptions; Apple App Store and Google Play process subscription payments; and, when you sign in with an external account, Google or Apple act as identity providers. These providers process your data only to provide their services and under our instructions.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep your account data while your account is active and for 30 days after your deletion request, after which it is permanently erased, except where we must keep it by law. Legal document acceptance records are kept immutably for audit purposes, with no retention limit.

## 6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. If you created a household, all members are unlinked and the household data is deleted along with your account. If you joined someone else's household, your profile is unlinked but the household remains for its other members. In all cases your tickets, budgets and personal data are deleted. Cancelling your paid subscription is handled separately from the app store.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export and delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can export your data from the app or contact us through the channel listed below to exercise any of these rights.

## 8. How We Protect Your Data

Data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS between your device and our servers. On your device, session tokens and sensitive data are stored encrypted via SecureStore (AES through the Android Keystore and iOS Keychain). On our servers, Supabase manages encryption at rest and strict access policies. We periodically audit our security practices. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to [security@ticketify.app].

## 9. International Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed on servers outside your country of residence where Supabase or our other providers operate. When we make such transfers we do so under standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms that ensure an adequate level of protection consistent with the GDPR, the LGPD and other applicable regulations.

## 10. Minors

Ticketify is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If a parent or guardian discovers that a minor has provided us with data without their consent, they can contact us through the channel listed below to request deletion.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when necessary. When there is a material change, we will notify you in the app and ask you to accept the updated version. If you continue using Ticketify after the notice, you are considered to have accepted the new version.

## 12. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or about how we process your data, email us at [contact@ticketify.app]. Please note this is a draft: the final version will be published after legal review.
